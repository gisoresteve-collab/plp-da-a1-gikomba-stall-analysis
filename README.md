## Project Overview

This project analyses 14 days of sales records and stock information from Wanjiku's fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. The objective is to evaluate overall business performance, compare weekly and daily sales patterns, identify products requiring restocking, calculate gross profit margins and provide practical recommendations to support better business decisions. The analysis uses core Python without pandas or NumPy.

## How to Run

Open the analysis notebook in Google Colab: [Open stall_analysis.ipynb in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/gisoresteve-collab/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

Alternatively, download `stall_analysis.ipynb` and open it using Google Colab.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Total sales:** KES 46,450 across 14 days, with average daily sales of KES 3,317.86.
2. **Weekly growth:** Sales increased by 10.66%, from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2.
3. **Weekend performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, which was 82.50% higher than the weekday average of KES 2,685.
